About Me

I think in systems.

Right out of college, I spent a year helping at-risk youth transition out of "the system" and into "the real world." During a training on the emotional challenges our residents faced, the instructor said, "It's not rocket science." He was right. It was harder.

That instinct still shapes how I work. I walk into messy, high-stakes systems, learn the domain, bring clarity, and help ship something useful. Today that shows up in healthcare workflows, enterprise identity, regulated systems, and AI-native B2B products.

I like the part of product work where a fuzzy problem becomes a clearer path forward through discovery, prototypes, requirements, stakeholder alignment, data, edge cases, and enough technical depth to make better product decisions.
